# Script: "Hackathon Starter Tips"

**Hook:** "Everyone at the hackathon started coding immediately. We opened Slides."
**Format:** UGC talking head + screen recordings + group chat screenshot
**Duration:** 60s
**Persona:** The Hackathon Newbie

---

**[0-3s] HOOK:**
"Everyone at the hackathon started coding immediately. We opened Slides."

*Visual: Talking head, dead serious face. Cut on "PowerPoint" for emphasis.*

---

**[3-8s] PROBLEM:**
"Here's what happens at every hackathon — teams start building right away, everyone wants their own feature, and by the end you've got a Frankenstein app with 5 half-working things and no story to tell the judges."

*Visual: Talking head, hands gesturing. Quick cuts to keep pace.*

---

**[8-20s] MECHANISM:**
"So here's what we did instead. First 4 hours — no one touched a single line of code. All 4 of us worked on the pitch. The slides. The script. The demo flow."

*Visual: Cut to group chat screenshot showing all 4 agreeing to do slides first. Zoom in on the funny parts.*

"Sounds crazy right? But this did two things — it forced all of us to actually agree on ONE core feature, and everyone understood exactly what we were building before we built it."

*Visual: Back to talking head. Hold up one finger on "ONE core feature."*

---

**[20-35s] PROOF:**
"Then when we finally started coding — hard-coded everything first. Mock data, fake API calls, the whole thing. Got the full demo flow looking clean, THEN split up and built the real components."

*Visual: Quick screen recording clips — terminal, Vercel deploy, code in Cloud Code.*

"Oh and the idea? I threw the problem statement into ChatGPT, got 5 ideas, told it to throw them all away and think harder. The judges called our idea 'very creative.'"

*Visual: Talking head, slight smirk on "throw them all away."*

---

**[35-45s] RESULT:**
"We won. And I went home and slept 7 hours during a 24-hour hackathon."

*Visual: Talking head, casual shrug. Maybe cut to a photo/clip from the hackathon win.*

---

**[45-50s] RECAP:**
"Pitch first. One feature. Hard-code the demo. Build the real thing last."

*Visual: Text overlay listing each point as it's said. Fast cuts.*

---

**[50-60s] CTA:**
"If you've got your first hackathon coming up, save this. You'll thank me later. Drop a comment if you want me to show my full Claude Code setup — I didn't even open VS Code."

*Visual: Talking head, pointing at camera on "save this."*

---

## Production Notes
- Group chat screenshot is the money shot — time it right after the "PowerPoint" reveal for maximum payoff
- Keep energy conversational, not preachy. You're telling a friend what worked.
- Screen recordings should be quick flashes (1-2s each), not tutorials
- The "slept 7 hours" line is the second hook — delivers the unexpected flex
